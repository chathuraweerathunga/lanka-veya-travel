# External platforms & links

All links are managed in **Admin → Settings & links**. Empty fields are hidden from visitors. Only add listings that genuinely belong to Lanka Veya Travel; the site never shows ratings, badges or reviews it hasn't been given.

| Platform | Field(s) | Where it appears | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tripadvisor | Listing URL, "write a review" URL | Footer, homepage review buttons | Claim the listing via Tripadvisor Management Center. Official widgets may only be embedded under Tripadvisor's widget terms; this site does not embed ratings by default. |
| Google | Business Profile URL, review link, Maps link | Footer, homepage, contact page (directions only if a Maps link is set) | Create/verify at business.google.com. Only add a Maps link once a real, verified location exists. Get the review link from your Business Profile ("Ask for reviews"). |
| Booking.com | Listing or approved affiliate URL | Footer | Booking.com lists accommodation; a transport/tour company may not have a property listing. Use an affiliate link only if approved in their Affiliate Partner Programme. No checkout is imitated or scraped. |
| Viator | Supplier listing / affiliate URL | Footer | Requires a supplier account (Viator Management Center) or affiliate approval. |
| GetYourGuide | Supplier listing / affiliate URL | Footer | Requires supplier onboarding or partner approval. |
| Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube | Profile URLs | Footer | — |

Bookings made on third-party platforms stay on those platforms; this site only links to them.

## Testimonials

Add genuine customer feedback in **Admin → Content → Testimonials** with the date received, its source (direct, Tripadvisor, Google…) and, where possible, a link to the original. Publishing requires confirming the feedback is genuine and that you have permission. The homepage shows the testimonials section only when at least one is published.

## Analytics

Optional, in *Settings → Analytics*: a Plausible domain (cookie-less) and/or a Google Analytics 4 ID. If you enable GA4, update the privacy policy and consider a consent banner where required.
